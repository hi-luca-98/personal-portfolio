Passionate, curious and friendly are some of the words I’d use to describe myself.

I’m currently studying a Bachelor of Data Science at Victoria University, where I also work as a Student Mentor and Library Student Assistant.

I’m interested in how organisations use technology and data as valuable resources to achieve their goals, and how insights gathered from data are communicated between stakeholders.

I also really enjoy collaborating with others. Through my role as a Student Mentor, I’ve had the opportunity to support and guide other students by sharing my educational and life experiences. I’ve found this really fulfilling and love being able to help others on their own journey.

Outside of study and work, I love playing sports. Soccer is my go-to, but I’m always keen to kick a footy or have a game of squash. I also enjoy cooking, trying out a nice restaurant, and going to the movies.

At the moment, I’m keen to expand my professional network and gain some industry experience through a work placement. I’m aware that I still have a lot to learn about the industry I’m hoping to enter, and I’m eager to learn from people with more experience than me and gain a better understanding of where I can contribute.
